Backyard Speed Development

Plyometric Speed Session

Build elastic power, quick feet, strong landings and a faster first step in a small space.

Space: 10 x 10 yards Time: about 30-35 min Equipment: rope, line/cones, light dumbbell
JUMP CLEAN
LAND STRONG
MOVE FASTER

1

Warm-Up - Choose One

Prepare the ankles, calves, hips and nervous system before explosive work.

OPTION 1

Jump Rope

  • 30 sec normal two-foot bounce
  • 20-30 sec left leg
  • 20-30 sec right leg
  • 30 sec rest
  • Repeat for 2 rounds

Cue: Light feet. Small jumps. Relax the shoulders.

Progression: Increase rhythm before increasing jump height.

OPTION 2

Pogo Progression

  • 2 x 10-12 two-leg pogos
  • 2 x 6-8 left-leg pogos
  • 2 x 6-8 right-leg pogos
  • Optional: 2 x 4-5 knee-drive hops each leg

Cue: Quick contact. Stay tall. Land quietly.

Rule: Only progress if the athlete stays balanced and springy.

2

Simple Plyometric Block

Keep these movements simple. The goal is quality, stiffness and control - not complicated choreography.

3 x 5

Squat Jump + Stick

Jump vertically, land softly and hold the landing for one second.

Cue: Explode up. Land quiet. Own the landing.

Progression: Remove the pause, then perform 2 controlled jumps together.

2 x 10-15 sec

Side-to-Side Line Hops

Use small two-foot jumps across a line. Keep the movement narrow and quick.

Cue: Feet together. Quick off the ground.

Progression: Increase speed before trying a single-leg version.

2 x 10-15 sec

Forward-Back Line Hops

Make small jumps forward and backward across a line with minimal ground time.

Cue: Small jumps. Fast contacts. Stay tall.

Progression: Add speed while keeping the jumps low.

2 x 6 each side

Split-Stance Switches

Start in a shallow split stance, make a small jump and switch the feet.

Cue: Stay centered. Switch quickly. Land under control.

Progression: Increase rhythm without turning it into a deep lunge.

3

Small-Space Speed Transfer

No long sprint is needed. These drills use only about 1-3 yards and focus on the first explosive movement.

4 reps

Fast Feet to First Step

Quick feet in place for 3-4 seconds, then explode forward for only 1-3 yards.

Cue: Fast feet - then one violent push.

Progression: Start on a clap or visual cue.

3-4 each side

Lateral Quick Feet to Push

Take 2-3 quick lateral steps, plant the outside foot and push hard into the opposite direction.

Cue: Low plant. Strong push. Win the first step.

Progression: Add a coach call for left or right.

4 reps

Pogo to First Step

Perform 4 quick pogos, land in an athletic position, then immediately push into the first acceleration step.

Cue: Spring - settle - explode.

Progression: Change the exit direction.

4

Dumbbell Power - Your 3 Activities

Use the attached activities as the loaded power block. Keep the weight light enough that the athlete can still move quickly and stay balanced.

Important: This is power work, not heavy strength work. Use controlled reps, low volume and full movement quality. Stop the set when speed or balance drops.
1

Dumbbell Activity 1

Loaded squat-style explosive movement with the weight held centrally.

Volume: 3 x 5

Focus: controlled load, strong push, stable base.

Progression: Keep the same load and improve speed before adding weight.

2

Dumbbell Activity 2

Single-leg loaded movement that challenges balance, hip control and push-off strength.

Volume: 2-3 x 5 each side

Focus: load the working leg, stay balanced and push the floor away.

Progression: Make each rep cleaner and faster before changing the load.

3

Dumbbell Activity 3

Quick loaded single-leg foot-contact movement with the weight controlled at the side.

Volume: 2-3 x 6-8 contacts

Focus: quick feet, quiet contact, stable torso.

Progression: Improve rhythm first. Do not add load if contacts become slow.

5
